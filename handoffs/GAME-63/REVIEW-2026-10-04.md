## Visual assessment
- **Batan**: credible small clustered village and field belt, but same-looking homes, repetitive crops and disproportionate farmland; chapel/inn/manor not conspicuous in standard review frame. No permanent village guildhall implied.
- **Albanes**: walls, towers, citadel and plausible contrasting wards are legible; precisely the guildhall/shops/taverns/inns are *present in data but invisible as named establishments*. Very regular near-circular enclosing wall and repeating plots make it synthetic.
- **Thilranlena**: coherent coastline-facing layout and piers, but generic blockwork, little harbour identity; the provider **synthesised** the shore and cannot claim it matches the GAME-62 Azgaar local coastline. Scene has water absent from city GeoJSON. Harbour semantics must not be fabricated from field geometry.

The maps need a clear layer for **a few important selectable establishments**, not roof icons or names on every building. Reuse Game-icons with restrained collision-aware labels. Wards/facilities need persistence owned by our own game schema; provider IDs stable within pinned layout but **not** canonical across engine upgrades; keep player discoveries separate from objective functions.

## Verified technical evidence & gates
- Original study reported 27 targeted upstream tests passed, source-input mapping checks, per-seed replay, malformed-source rejection (21 negatives across three maps). One initial strict port/water assertion failed, honestly reported: city GeoJSON omits water. Tests do *not* prove broad seed quality, accurate geographical fit, feature-ID stability across versions or dark-fantasy style consistency.
- GPL-3.0-only engine licence requires an explicit project distribution decision before vendoring/using in shipping game. Artwork has separate licence conditions. Don't treat original package version 3.0.1 and development preview as identical.
- GAME-62 PR #50 remains the separate local-region review gate. No GAME-63 integration authorised.
- Next isolated research/visual follow-up: [GAME-67](https://linear.app/marksprietsma/issue/GAME-67) tests persistent establishment schema and selectable labels on these exact maps; research **only**, not NPCs, economy, trading or gameplay.

**Recommendation**: retain Settlemaker as an optional prospective provider, but **conditional adaptation required** for geographical/scale coherence and aesthetic fit; demonstrate functional premises and stable identities before deciding to integrate.
