# Game-icons attribution

Icons by **Delapouite and Lorc**, from [Game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net/) and the [Game-icons source repository](https://github.com/game-icons/icons), licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

Prepared for The Road Has Gone Dark by removing opaque backgrounds and recolouring the original foreground geometry to parchment ink. Original paths are retained. World and local maps share these assets.

Detailed source roles and author mappings for the landmark icons are retained in `landmark_sources.json` (upstream commit `82d948812bfe3f269ef8f731dcdb07b08160edc4`). The selected settlement family and original source URL are also recorded in `assets/map_icons/trials/candidate_manifest.json`.
