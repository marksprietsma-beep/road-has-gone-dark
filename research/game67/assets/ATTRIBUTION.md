# Research marker artwork

These are unchanged copies of the project's selected Game-icons assets.
Source registry: `assets/map_icons/trials/game-icons/landmark_sources.json` and
`assets/map_icons/trials/README.md` at accepted GAME-62 baseline.

Game-icons, by **Delapouite and Lorc**, [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/),
[game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net/), original upstream source revision
`82d948812bfe3f269ef8f731dcdb07b08160edc4`.
The project's prior adaptation removed opaque backdrops and recoloured ink;
this research copies the prepared geometry without further changes.
`unidentified.svg` is the project's neutral authored fallback.

Symbols convey broad visual categories, not an invented exact source glyph:
inn/tavern → inns; shop/warehouse → trading; smithy → mine (workplace proxy);
guildhall → capital (major civic premises proxy); chapel → monastery;
manor/guardhouse → fort; stable → village; pier → canoes; well → water-sources;
mill → hamlet. Labels and the detail panel provide exact semantics. These
reused icons are a research shortlist, not final facility-specific art approval.
