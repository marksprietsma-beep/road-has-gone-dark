## Scale measurements versus guidelines

**Measured/source-backed:** burg population values, flags/group, exact polygon area
in square source coordinates, provider building counts and provider-local art bounds.
**Source-relative:** within-world population percentile, population ratios and exact
cell/window proportions. **Aesthetic only:** footprint envelopes and clearing budgets.
None establish kilometres, hex travel distances or people per actual hectare.

The prototype uses `0.02 × sqrt(population / 0.306)`, bounded to **0.8–12% of
that parent's polygon area**. The Batan reference and limits are explicit aesthetic
choices, not empirical demographic laws or new canonical classifications. A port
is a character overlay, not necessarily the largest category. Albanes is a capital
yet has lower source population than Thilranlena; importance and size are separate.
Capja is a genuine town; no detailed map is claimed for it.

Fractions compare different parent cells. The resulting illustrative areas are about
2.63 / 7.30 / 9.66 / 4.18 square source units, respectively, **before clipping**.
Thilranlena's smaller source cell limits its budget even though its population is
larger. The tint discs are clipped to owned cell polygons; at coastal burgs that
reduces actual displayed area. Treat these as budget illustrations, not fitted
settlement footprints or an accepted future rendering policy. Full dry-land,
burg-to-burg overlap and shape-fit constraints need any future footprint consumer.

Recommended character: village small rural footprint; ordinary town broader developed
area and source approaches; capital prominent infrastructure and fortifications only
when flags/output support them; port shore-facing development and actual known
maritime structures. Farms/forest remain local artistic detail. Exact roads, owned
cell/water masks and real neighbours constrain illustration before finer decoration.

Raw provider bounds cannot be pasted into source coordinates. Batan's preserved
frame is about 506.5×559.4 provider units; Albanes about 223.5×223.5; Thilranlena
about 226.2×179.4. Their parent-cell extents are roughly a few to tens of source
units. The mismatch proves **unit incompatibility**, not that Batan is truly larger
than its cell. Village output describes local metres; city scale evidence uses a
population heuristic. Neither calibrates Azgaar's canvas or regional hexes.
Current navigation fits these independent scenes to the screen; it does not already
place their entire art frames on the region at a common physical scale.
