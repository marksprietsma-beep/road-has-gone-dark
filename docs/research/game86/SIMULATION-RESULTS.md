## Interpretation before selection

- Win rates measure these fixed kits under one public-state greedy policy. They do not rank the final classes, price utility, or establish human enjoyment. The simulations omit saving throws, real stealth, directional cover, escape, build progression and most spell choices.
- A permits one ordinary attack with movement; B permits two full attacks while stationary; C permits three with penalties. They are complete economy packages. C's shorter rounds and fewer walking-only activations do not prove shorter wall-clock play or better choices.
- Most party rows favour the fighter/rogue/mage side, but the opposite side has a controller whose policy spends actions on unreliable-value Slow. That composition/controller confound precludes attributing party advantage to rogue or mage strength.
- Slow vs none below is a counterexample to treating more seeded runs as AI validation: using Slow correlates with *more opponent wins* in this policy. Stun denies actual activations, but its net effect also includes foregone attacks and altered paths. No per-spell damage-equivalent claim is justified.
- Spatial mirroring uses different odd/even seeds, not a same-seed side/initiative crossover. Keyed attack ordinals also cease to represent the same event once policies diverge. Paired comparisons reduce sampling noise without eliminating these biases.
- Wilson intervals cover seeded sampling only. Geometry has 32 seeds per cell; scale6 has 32 per configuration, so fine distinctions are particularly uncertain. Duplicate baselines in different families are reproducibility checks, not independent replication.

## Counterexamples and exact arithmetic

| Lane diagnostic | Contact turn | Shots before contact |
|---|---:|---:|
| unbounded_no_tools | None | 39 |
| bounded_no_tools | 4 | 3 |
| unbounded_dash | 3 | 2 |
| unbounded_charge | None | 39 |
| unbounded_both | 2 | 1 |

The unbounded equal-speed lane preserves an endless firing/retreat cycle without approach tools. A finite board can hide it. Dash sacrifices attacks to gain ground; charge requires a straight clear lane. These tools do not guarantee safe contact against superior movement, blockers or coordinated ranged units.

The illusion observation diagnostic changes route length from 10 to 6 after one observer spends a main action investigating. Another observer still believes the wall. A decoy attack spends its action before learning the target is false. This proves a narrow information/command boundary, not class balance or damage prevented.

Exact d20 enumeration gives +7 vs AC15 a 65% hit chance and 5.4 expected damage at base8/+4 critical. Two independent misses occur with probability 12.25%. A 34HP fighter therefore takes roughly 6.30 such attacks to lose 34HP by expectation; this ratio is not the expected stopping time, and real focus-fire/reactions change it.

One automatic full-activation stun removes one-third of a three-member team's activations, versus one-sixth with six members. A 1.5× CT activation rate combined with 1.5× movement yields 2.25× traversal per reference time. These are arithmetic warnings, not tested CT or campaign simulations.

## Decision and remaining evidence gates

**Recommend A: movement + one main action, one shared reaction, bounded approach tools, and telegraphed limited magic.** This is a readability and interaction choice supported by viable approach behavior, not an optimization for a target win rate. B rewards stationary double attacks; C makes movement less costly but adds attack-penalty bookkeeping and still produced dense-terrain stalls.

Before class tuning: add same-seed side/initiative crossover, at least a conservative and a coordinating policy, Guard/protect/escape decisions, and per-turn regret inspection. Before shipping: human tests on 3v3 and selected 6v6 layouts, directional cover, actual observation-restricted illusions/stealth, saving throws, resources across encounters, and save/replay of the same engine. These are additional acceptance gates, not omitted scheduled experiments.

See the [actual seeded 3v3 command walkthrough](WALKTHROUGH.md), including a telegraphed burst that hits nobody.
