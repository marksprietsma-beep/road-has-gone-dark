## Human Windows review

1. Download the linked Actions artifact, extract its outer ZIP, then extract `game133-spell-repertoires-windows-x64.zip`. Keep all files/folders together and launch `road-has-gone-dark.exe`. Neither Godot nor Node installation is required.
2. New Game → generate/select world → origin → hometown → create three characters, including Sorcerer (CHA) and Druid (WIS). Existing saves retain their original pin and do not gain v18 automatically.
3. Character Sheet → Spellbook. Search by name/tier. Sorcerer knowledge is permanent; learn new choices only within the earned known-spell allowance. Druid chooses prepared copies within tier capacity. Read real score requirements and descriptions; Save spell selections at home.
4. Complete ordinary opportunities, return home and advance explicitly. Tier4 is Sorcerer8/Druid7; tiers5/6/7 are Sorcerer10/12/13 and Druid9/11/13. Effective hybrid casting levels count, not total level alone. Selecting/preparing does not replenish shared slots; an eligible expedition return/rest does.
5. In battle choose a spell and a highlighted legal target. Crystal reservoir adds a nonstacking buffer; Bramble tether can root an enemy; Slipstream veil changes weapon concealment and stride; Packward canopy protects living allies/creatures; Predator's rhythm changes actual reach/stride/weapon damage. Hostile area spells affect enemy targets only. Saves/resistance and finite action/slot/copy costs remain authoritative.
6. Save, exit the application, relaunch and continue. Verify identities, known/prepared choices, HP/resources, owned companions and advancement. Production has no high-level fixture or free-XP button. Higher-tier demonstration captures are explicitly labelled development fixtures.

Existing advancement initialises newly owned resource pools at capacity; it does not refill existing spent pools. A separately labelled backend fixture applies a real Crystal reservoir cost, uses the existing trusted runtime transition and sole writer, and proves saving known choices preserves the depleted resources without touching the original earned campaign. It is not a claim of playable out-of-combat casting. Ordinary saved encounter casts and return/rest are verified separately.
