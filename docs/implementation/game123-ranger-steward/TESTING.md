# Ranger / Beast Steward review steps

## Native Windows review

The v32 executable has matching-source native Windows acceptance. [DELIVERY.md](DELIVERY.md) provides the exact artifact, source and inner-ZIP SHA-256; do not substitute the preserved31 release.

Download the outer GitHub Windows artifact, extract it, then extract the inner `ranger-steward-windows-x64.zip`. Keep the entire directory and launch `road-has-gone-dark.exe`; no installed Godot, Node or artwork is required. Escape skips the intro. Use a fresh New Game for v32 mechanics. Existing saved games retain their original exact pins and behavior; do not delete or migrate them to test compatibility.

1. New Game → generate/select world → origin → hometown → party creator. Select Ranger. Use the existing Attributes, Skills, Feats, Equipment, Spells, Progression and Specialisation controls. Prestige classes must not appear as starting-class options.
2. Create deliberately different Rangers: Archery with Bow/Leathers; Blade with Shortblade/Leathers; a prospective companion steward. Choosing Blade does not silently change equipment—select the actual compatible weapon explicitly. Pick a permanent quarry (raiders or hostile archers), legal feats and skill ranks. Inspect the real character sheet.
3. Prepare the first adventure, follow a source-backed opportunity/lead, travel and enter combat. Aimed shot requires an actual ranged weapon and both main action and movement; it grants+2 accuracy and costs no resolve. A melee character must see the unavailable action, no legal target highlight and no successful forged intent. Paid Archery style consumes a main and tracker resolve, gives the authored training bonuses, and adds damage only to actual ranged attacks. It does not strengthen a fallback melee departure reaction.
4. Earn XP through ordinary opportunities; return home and explicitly fund levels. At Ranger 4 choose a permanent companion. The animal retains its owner-derived identity and definition, has an actual turn in the existing order, and can move/attack/perform its supplied special action. Target highlights, HP, movement, attack and reaction costs use the original board/rules. Animal artwork remains a clearly labelled humanoid fallback.
5. For a Steward route, allocate at least 4 Animal Handling ranks and 3 Nature ranks; choose Beast stewardship after acquiring the permanent nature bond. A legal Ranger 5 with250 earnedXP may manually select Beast Steward or decline and remain Ranger. Qualification never changes class automatically. Select Swift or Enduring training. Commands cost the owner's main and a finite command; Rally heals/guards a legal ally, Pack Drive supports actual ally attack/Will and buffers. Owned pets retain their own movement, main, reaction and effort pools.
6. Prepare actual Ranger nature spells with legal Wisdom and copies. Cast control/damage/recovery spells; observe a save, legal target/range/line of sight, shared tier slot and individual copy cost. Spellcasting competes with weapon/command actions. Empty copies/pools deny use; eligible home recovery restores capacities without XP.
7. After combat, return to regional play/home. A defeated companion remains downed during growth until eligible recovery. Ordinary living damage deficits and spent creature effort survive advancement. Temporary summons keep separate actor identities and finite durations; they do not become permanent companions. Existing owner-defeat withdrawal occurs at the next global-round boundary, preserving the pet record.
8. Fully exit, relaunch and resume. Verify the same character IDs, ordered class/choice history, companion identity/definition, HP, resources, active battle, logs and next deterministic command. Previously pinned campaigns must still load without adopting32.
