# GAME-33 integration contract

GAME-32 supplies validated characters, not tactical battles. Consume exact-pinned registry definitions and `RulesCharacter.derive_character(record).snapshot` as authority input. Reject unavailable/mismatched rules; do not recalculate classes in a controller or renderer. Snapshots include build/snapshot hashes, derived statistics, legal/prepared ability IDs, casting, senses, equipment, capacities/current HP/resources, statuses/maintained descriptors and the action contract. Save current mechanics through a validated campaign preview under the existing guard/journal. Persist advancement separately from current mechanics.

GAME-33 owns battle identities/tiles, board geometry, move/reach/range legality, LOS/cover/engagement, initiative and fixed individual activations, hit eligibility, target ownership/friendly fire, pending effects, death/incapacitation, objectives and observer knowledge. Apply the pack’s engaged-ranged modifier only after detecting actual engagement. Dash/Charge/Disengage are declarations to interpret, not completed movement implementations. Guard/Precision/departure triggers and once-per-activation restrictions require authoritative events.

Use one move token, one main token and one shared reaction. Carry remaining reaction through activations; call the explicit global-round refresh once at that boundary. Validate costs before resolving. Call pure `use_ability`/effect primitives with explicit RNG and event IDs/context; they return candidate records and structured events. Invalid calls yield no partial commit. `RulesRng.attack_roll` resolves only the numeric contest/critical flag; GAME-33 chooses an eligible weapon/target and applies returned damage metadata. Manual and future AI controllers must submit commands to this same authority path.

Save Focus between encounters and only refresh definitions at their declared scope. Expiring statuses requires the explicit declared boundary. Delayed Burst returns a paid intent with effects/timing/locked tile/interruption metadata; GAME-33 schedules/resolves/cancels it. A deferred intent does not itself apply damage or store a pending battlefield effect. Maintained illusion duration, break conditions and observer beliefs also belong to that consumer.

Illusions have no physical collision. Keep physical authority IDs and observer apparent IDs separate. Give real and illusory perceived objects the same public reference vocabulary, and expose only the projection that observer is entitled to see. Public appearance omits kind/source/physical/disbelief truth. The trusted Echo hook emits an authority intent, not an already spawned entity or changed observer belief. Senses/bypass/investigation/saves are declared content to enforce.

Do not copy isolated GAME-86 win rates into class balancing. Test protected ranged positions, screening, objectives, information and mixed teams in the actual battlefield implementation. GAME-34 AI and GAME-35 autoresolve remain unstarted; GAME-32 adds no special auto bonuses or alternate definitions.
