FINAL THREE PRESTIGE CAREERS — WINDOWS x64 REVIEW

Extract the outer GitHub artifact ZIP, then final-prestige-windows-x64.zip. Keep the complete extracted folder together. Launch road-has-gone-dark.exe. No Godot, Node or separately installed artwork is required. Escape skips the intro. Existing campaigns retain their original rules pins: do not delete or recreate them. Start a separate new campaign for v35 mechanics.

Create three characters: Human Wizard (INT15), Human Rogue (DEX15, INT14), Dwarf Fighter (INT15 for supporting ranks). Keep ancestry separate from culture/background. Prestige careers never appear as starting classes. Select the relevant prerequisites deliberately: Knight needs Weapon Focus and Spellcraft4 plus BAB4 and actual permanent arcane tier3; Shadow needs prior level5, Stealth5 and Dodge; Defender needs Dwarf ancestry, BAB5 and Toughness. Check each career's displayed requirements: there are no new mandatory mentors or quests.

Follow ordinary opportunities, complete objectives, return home and manually commit earned advancement. No level or XP grants are present. The verified Knight route is Wizard5/Fighter2/Knight5. Shadow is Rogue5/Shadow10. Defender is Fighter5/Defender10. Entry thresholds are490XP for Knight,250XP for the others. Eligibility is optional: continue your base class instead to decline. Only a deliberate funded commit adds a prestige level. All characters can reach total20; supported prestige caps are Knight5, Shadow10 and Defender10.

KNIGHT: Choose your actual existing arcane parent. At Knight3/5 choose a spell channel or extra battle reserve. Prepare/know the original spell normally. Equip a proficient single melee weapon (the Wizard's staff works); channel spends the original spell slot/prepared copy and main action. It delivers the original spell only after an actual weapon hit. A miss still spends the slot. Bow, ally, unknown spell, exhausted pool and suppressed-body attempts are illegal. Spellguard strike adds a short ward; Ready arcane riposte spends a main action and reserve before a possible incoming-melee-miss counter. Instant spellguard spends the shared reaction and two reserve uses, preserving main/move. That reaction cannot also fund a counter or opportunity attack.

SHADOW: Shadow shroud costs a main action/use. Slipping shroud instead costs movement/use and leaves main available; it does not itself teleport or count as having moved. Unmasking focus grants temporary true sight, bypassing finite weapon doubles. Umbral double costs a main action/two uses and absorbs one ordinary weapon attempt before accuracy. Spells and true sight remain counterplay. Shrouds are short weapon miss chances, not invisibility or immunity. Effects expire normally; all supernatural activations are suppressed in magic-suppressed forms.

DEFENDER: Anchored guard spends main/move; Ready bulwark spends movement while preserving main. Both root the character. Hold line adds one real melee reach with moderate mitigation. Stone redoubt trades that extended reach for higher defence/resistance. Stances are exclusive, temporary and paid from a finite pool. An equipped, living anchored Defender can spend its shared reaction to counter an incoming melee miss; arrows do not trigger it. Movement stays illegal even if a later turn restores the move token. End turn remains visible at the left footer after reaching0/0.

Continue through combat, return home, save, quit the executable and relaunch. The same identity, ancestry, ordered history, chosen parent/channel, resources, statuses and deterministic battle must remain. Eligible home rest restores reserves/slots without granting XP.

Native evidence distinguishes real1960XP/total15 adventures (all three full prestige tracks, with deliberate entry/decline forks) from labelled total20 development fixtures.50-career technical acceptance is not final balance or human usability approval. Existing humanoid LPC anatomy fallback and long-label/small-window clipping remain; no new custom sprite anatomy, teleportation or exploration stealth is claimed.
