# Remaining character-completeness gaps after this bounded wave

This cohort adds ten spell identities, not a complete spell catalogue or ten new effect opcodes. Existing upper-tier scaled templates are retained; Wizard8–9 and Cleric8–9 remain thin. Druid, Sorcerer, Beguiler, Spirit Shaman, Bard and other supported casting tracks still need individual repertoire audits and original executable choices. Wizard/Cleric1–3 are unchanged. Current spell learning is existing class-tier ownership plus selected preparations/known repertoires, not a new scroll/study economy.

Unravel enchantment removes an explicit fourteen-status boon list, not arbitrary magic or ongoing world curses. Purifying renewal cleanses an explicit sixteen-status tactical affliction list. Spell suppression blocks spell-tagged abilities only, not powers, supernatural effects, weapons or terrain interactions. Return to breath recovers a downed persistent ally while battle remains active; it is not resurrection of a permanently dead character or a way to revive expired summons. Existing save-half/evasion and resistance semantics remain the project rules; no new spell-resistance or SRD spell framework is introduced.

Current ordinary raider AI primarily uses weapon attacks. Boon-dispelling and hostile spell suppression have controlled caster/boon fixtures rather than a claim of rich naturally occurring enemy-caster matchups. This wave does not redesign AI or add unrelated encounters. Most damage and defence choices already affect ordinary raider combat.
