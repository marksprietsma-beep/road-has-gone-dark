# Windows manual review

Use the download and checksum in DELIVERY.md once native release verification is complete. Extract the outer GitHub artifact ZIP, then the playable ZIP inside it. Keep its folders together and launch `road-has-gone-dark.exe`. Godot and Node do not need installing. Escape skips the introduction.

1. Choose **New Game**, select/generate a world, origin and hometown, then create a Warlock, Binder and Dragonfire Adept. New campaigns use v21 automatically. Continuing an older campaign deliberately keeps its old rules, so use a fresh campaign to review these changes.
2. In the normal creator, inspect the permanent specialisations. Warlock chooses **Binding invocation** or **Ward invocation**; Binder chooses **Embers** or **Sentinel**; Dragonfire chooses an element and an invocation. Confirm each adventurer and enter regional play. Character sheets should show the actual owned abilities and resources; an unchosen covenant/invocation is unavailable.
3. Follow an opportunity to combat. **Binding utterance** spends a main action but no pool; a successful Will save prevents its movement penalty. **Dusk veil**, on the alternative Warlock build, clears Weakened and grants weapon concealment. At-will does not mean multiple main actions in one activation.
4. **Embers** spends a commitment for saved fire damage and Off Balance. **Covenant intercession**, on a Sentinel Binder, targets an ally within3, grants temporary HP and clears Weakened. Its Guarding protects an adjacent *other* ally using the recipient's existing shared reaction. It grants no extra reaction and does not force enemies to attack a particular person. Inspect the rest-limited pool and combat log.
5. A Dragonfire Adept's basic elemental breath remains repeatable without spending invocation resolve. Selected ward/veil/hindrance actions spend that finite reserve instead. Select an action and hover a legal target: **Affects:** lists the actual affected combatants. Walls, legal centre/range and enemy-versus-ally targeting apply; this is radius targeting, not a cone.
6. Earn normal opportunity XP, return home and use **Characters → Advance**. Level6 requires250 cumulative XP. At Dragonfire level6, deliberately choose the matching element's **lance** or **wide breath**. The lance has range6, one target and two global-round-start recovery boundaries; during recovery its button is disabled even with a fresh main action. Basic breath remains usable. Wide breath has range3/radius2 and spends one resolve for the whole enemy burst, with a separate Reflex save per target.
7. Resolve combat, return home and rest. Earned results persist, rewards cannot be duplicated, and eligible rest restores finite commitments/resolve. Save, fully exit and relaunch, then resume: the same selections, advancement and saved combat resources should remain. Do not delete old saves or manually change their rules pins.

Later Warlock Pressure, Binder spirit/form powers and full level20 combinations are demonstrated by explicitly labelled development fixtures. The genuine automated campaign earns490XP and reaches8; it does not claim ordinary earned level20 or provide a production XP button.

Production scenes remain `scenes/ui/character_sheet.tscn` and `scenes/combat/first_adventure.tscn`, entered through the existing journey. The review harness is `tests/supernatural_signatures/review.tscn`; use its Windows workflow/prepared fixtures rather than opening the raw scene with no environment or campaign handoff.
