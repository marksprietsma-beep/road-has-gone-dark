The original pre-fix native reproduction is retained separately; current historical25 continuation preserves old Cognition range while receiving the accurate adjacent-only caption.

Production scenes: `res://scenes/ui/party_creation.tscn` and `res://scenes/combat/first_adventure.tscn`. Launch the complete game through New Game/Resume to populate world, party and campaign context.

1. Extract the outer GitHub ZIP, then its inner playable ZIP. Keep all helper/art/licence/support files beside `road-has-gone-dark.exe`; run it without Godot/Node installed. Escape skips intro.
2. Fresh New Game defaults to26. Continue an old campaign and verify its exact original rules remain pinned. Never reset or migrate a save for this test.
3. In the ordinary creator choose Psychic Warrior or Divine Mind. Build > Specialisation/Powers shows persistent options. Warrior may select damage or support powers; Divine Mind requires a selected mantle and aura doctrine. Out-of-mantle/high-tier powers are unavailable and explained. Cognition Adept must never be a starting class.
4. Review sheets and known powers. In combat choose augmentation deliberately: higher expenditure stays within manifesting level, consumes one main action and actual shared PP. Arcane multiclass spells still use separate slots and their own attributes. Weapon augmentation and selected ally support have different effects.
5. Divine Mind: begin an aura for one main/resolve. Living allies within3 orthogonal tiles and clear sight receive its typed bonus. Moving outside, blocking sight, downing or transforming the source removes the projection. Maintaining it has no per-turn resource cost. A later action can cast a power while the aura continues, or switch aura for another main/resolve. Rest restores reserves/removes rest-duration aura. This is not Bard performance upkeep.
6. Earn real opportunity XP, return home and explicitly commit advancement. Meet permanent Cognition prerequisites (level6, INT13, BAB4, Perception4, Psychic Discipline and genuine psychic access); eligible characters may choose it or continue a base class. Eligibility never assigns it automatically. It has five prestige levels. Its direct psychic damage precedes a separate weapon roll that may miss; equipped melee and exact adjacency are required even with larger ordinary reach. Adjacent captions must say range1.
7. Existing natural forms suppress psychic actions and equipment while allowing actual natural attacks; reversion restores the same identity/gear. No transformed manifesting or custom creature art is claimed.
8. Deplete reserves, cross encounters, save, exit the process, relaunch and Resume. Check exact choices, multiclass history, resources, statuses, active combat and next-command/RNG replay. Return home for eligible rest; neither reload nor a selector can create XP or refill reserves.
