# Sequential engineering quality review

Reviewed the final pack’s first 50 states, 100 regions and 200 hometowns in cross-world round-robin source-ID order. The full reproducible corpus is [SEQUENTIAL-REVIEW.md](SEQUENTIAL-REVIEW.md); this is engineering review, not Mark’s acceptance.

The review found and corrected indefinite articles, repeated institutional phrasing, generic parent-dependency wording, and incompatible regional role/economy combinations. A grazing district now requires pastoral husbandry; a cultivated district requires farming or grain work. Regional supply describes the actual generated product rather than claiming every district supplies its parent’s two headline industries.

States differ in political posture, two economic families, social custom and outward orientation. Provinces differ in source-compatible role, livelihood and contribution. Towns differ in work, regional contribution and public custom, retaining the state’s social character. Memory/tradition remains secondary. In particular, a coastal hometown can have a farming livelihood; the separate factual panel still identifies its source-backed port/coast. This does not turn every port into a shipyard.

Across seven worlds: 149 states, 1,482 regions and 3,031 eligible hometowns. Normalising away place/culture/parent names gives 149 distinct state descriptions, 884 regional descriptions and 1,719 hometown descriptions. Distinct fact signatures are 149 / 1,235 / 2,225; the most repeated signature appears 1 / 6 / 8 times. These numbers count substantive selected fields rather than proper-name variation.

Remaining limits: common phrases and modest customs intentionally recur. This is a readable V2 identity layer, not a history simulation or unlimited narrative generator. Agricultural, craft and woodland communities share several mundane practices. State prose refers to mapped cultural presence, not demographics, inherited ethnicity, language or a census. Parent economy is a generated narrative focus, not proof of every local industry or a working trade simulation. There are no gameplay advantages, danger ratings or promises of available encounters.

Automated batch validation checks all records; sequential reading found no unresolved essential geography contradiction. Actual Godot layout/input and native distribution verification are reported separately in the final validation record.
