# Human quality review

The first 50 sequential leads, first 50 sequential site histories and first 50 generated choice expectations were read without cherry-picking. Examples span the 105 actual source regions in `batch-samples.json`. They distinguish watchtowers, workshops, record halls, chapels, refuges, communal yards and agricultural buildings; they preserve source identity and keep undiscovered rumour names/coordinates private.

The tone is restrained and understandable. Public appearance is separated from later history. Fiction is explicitly generated, not represented as canonical Azgaar history. Conservative exclusion of water/road/mining claims avoids the more serious geographic contradictions. No fabricated threat, protection, currency, monster or mechanical promise is used.

Repetition is material: 339 distinct appearance strings / 840 records means 501 repeats, and the three lead goal forms recur. Prefixes repeat across cells; the generic rumour account is deliberately vague. “Its history records that it…” sometimes assigns agency to a building rather than its occupants; the intended historical event remains understandable, but prose should be improved in a later content-authoring version. These are not hidden by selecting unusually good examples.

Choices change persistent knowledge, investigation status, further leads and costs. Role/background options use actual party fields rather than invented checks. Withdrawal remains meaningfully unresolved. A self-review found Survey claiming a further rumour even when already known: it now uses an ordinary perimeter-record message unless it actually adds that rumour. The actual outcome corpus explicitly regression-tests this case. Its first 50 sequential transactions were read: examination records the generated use, surveying records the perimeter, and withdrawal preserves visited/unresolved state without charging investigation costs. The two provision starting states correctly produce different remaining supplies with the same action costs. These intentionally repeated developer fixtures test transactions; they are not a prose-diversity benchmark.

The actual screenshot sequence was inspected at all three resolutions: titles and prose are legible; lists/details scroll; Return home and Main menu stay outside the scroll pane; discovery adds a marker without exposing unknown sites. The original illustration's large settlement symbols obscure some terrain at 640×360; this is retained and documented rather than claimed solved. Screenshots are captured after font-atlas/layout settling, and pixel assertions verify that title, origin reminder and saved-state status actually render. Keyboard focus follows offscreen choices in the detail pane. Laptop Windows visual acceptance and human session pacing are still for Mark's review.
