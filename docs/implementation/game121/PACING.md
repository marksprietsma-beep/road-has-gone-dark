# GAME-121 progression pacing

The existing core builder policy stays `10 × (level−1)²` cumulative XP. The new versioned objective policy grants10 XP to each participating character for a fully successful report, including peaceful/recovery/investigation outcomes. Party members receive parallel rewards, not three times the reward for one character. A four-report set awards at most40 XP; partial/failed/withdrawn reports reduce it.

| Target character level | Total XP | Full successes from zero | Four-report sets at perfect success |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2 | 10 | 1 | 1 |
| 3 | 40 | 4 | 1 |
| 6 | 250 | 25 | 7 |
| 12 | 1,210 | 121 | 31 |
| 20 | 3,610 | 361 | 91 |

This removes the hard20-XP ceiling. It does not establish human pacing as balanced. The ordinary automated service journey earned380 XP from40 reports (38 full successes), reached Rogue2/Wizard3/ArcaneTrickster2, and took473.731 seconds in this cloud environment. That includes real transactions and combat commands but skips human reading, build decisions and most presentation pauses. It is not an eight-minute human career or a measured player-time forecast. Native Linux earned390 XP in another seeded campaign; failures and available combat vary with saved context.

At baseline10 XP, level6 requires a tractable first run of25 successful objectives, but121/361 successes for12/20 may become repetitive with the present seven activity templates. Human playtests should record time per noncombat objective/fight, proportion of repeated templates, failure rate, number of interesting build decisions and whether encounters remain meaningful. Do not change the XP curve or multiply rewards merely to shorten an automation benchmark.

New opponents use legal progression through the existing character kernel at the rounded-up mean party level, capped12. Their basic AI and one/two-member groups remain limited. Higher class powers, broader enemies, encounters beyond tier12 and a richer activity pool need separately verified batches. A later scaling policy, if justified, must receive a new immutable version rather than changing current or historical rewards.

The first prestige paths have different gates. Demonstrated histories enter Arcane Trickster at250 XP (character6), Duelist/Mystic Theurge at360 (character7), Eldritch Knight at490 (character8). These are legally validated example histories, not a promise that any build qualifies at those levels.

Existing engine1/2 campaigns retain their original20×(level−1)² policy and policy hash; new objective supply can sustain those campaigns too. Old noncombat results remain0 XP, and old completed battles never receive another award. The sustainable loop is a dependency for further character content, not completion of core13–20 or the403/66/~283 programme.
