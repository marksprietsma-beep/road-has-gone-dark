# Finite character-milestone exit

GAME-123 covers the existing50 supported careers. The760 research occurrences remain a provenance ledger, not a prerequisite to entering living-world development. No added class/race/spell count is necessary to close a bounded original identity. Human Windows readability/balance acceptance remains separate from automated functional readiness.

Recommended next cohort: **Ardent, Lurk and Erudite**. Reuse the existing known-power, augmentation, mantle, veil/ambush and distinct-per-rest implementations. Reproduce actual defects before adding mechanics. Ardent needs durable mantle eligibility and meaningful tactical alternatives; Lurk needs action/resource/veil/adjacent-hit boundaries; Erudite needs distinct-identity limits across augmentation, encounter changes, rest and cold restart. Choose the strongest2–4 concrete deficiencies, demonstrate existing working functionality, and stop when those defining contracts have current native controls, costs, positive/negative tactical and exact persistence evidence. No new sourcebook roster.

The evidence-gated matrix's remaining acceptance is consolidated into four finite groups:

1. Psychic/resource consumers: Psychic Warrior, Ardent, Lurk, Erudite, Divine Mind, Cognition Adept, Metamind and Pyrokineticist. Check strike versus augmentation, mantles, distinct-rest identities, veil, auras and finite advanced reserves.
2. Remaining caster/hybrid restrictions: Sorcerer, Druid, Duskblade, Archmage, Mystic Theurge and Abjurant Champion. Close supported high-tier repertoire and channel/path interactions; require genuine targets, costs/counterplay and independent casting tracks, not complete tabletop spell conversion.
3. Martial/divine/equipment/path consumers: Barbarian, Fighter, Monk, Paladin, Ranger, Eldritch Knight, Shadowdancer and Dwarven Defender. Close the specifically advertised rage/ki/divine, protection, gear and conditional path triggers, including exhaustion and expiry.
4. Creature/body-dependent paths: Dragon Disciple, Warshaper, Bonded Summoner, Beast Steward and Grove Warden. Demonstrate actual ownership, actions/growth/limits, form-dependent benefits and equipment/reversion. Do not advertise unsupported flight, aquatic travel or world transformations.

These are bounded acceptance groups, not instructions to implement every book. Split only when distinct mechanisms genuinely need independent review. Accept existing implementations once demonstrated; do not create a new content wave merely because a level grants a numerical improvement. Preserve every pinned save and the single GAME-32/campaign authority.

Exit after the50-career matrix has no material defining functional gap, usable Windows controls and exact persistence are demonstrated, and Mark's explicit visual/balance review and GAME-123 acceptance are recorded. Then GAME-142 and GAME-141 can integrate real monsters/NPCs, settlements/factions and living-world encounters. Neither project is implemented during GAME-152.
